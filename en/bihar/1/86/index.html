«8»- l, al-Khisal; li, the Amali of al-Saduq: Ibn al-Barqi, from his father, from his grandfather, from 'Amr b. 'Uthman, from Abu Jamila (1), from Ibn Tarif (2), from Ibn Nubata (3), from 'Ali b. Abi Talib, peace be upon them both, who said: Gabriel descended upon Adam, peace be upon him, and said: Adam, I have been commanded to give you a choice of one of three; so choose one and leave two. Adam said to him: And what are the three, Gabriel? He said: Intellect, modesty and religion (4). Adam said: Then I have chosen the intellect. So Gabriel said to modesty and religion: Depart. And he called him; and they said to him: Gabriel, we have been commanded (5) to be with the intellect wherever it is. He said: Then to your business. And he ascended.

sn, al-Mahasin: 'Amr b. 'Uthman, the like of it. Elucidation: al-sha'n, with the hamza, is the affair and the state — that is, keep to your business, or your business is with you. Perhaps the purpose was to alert Adam, peace be upon him, and his children to the greatness of the blessing of the intellect. It has been said that the discourse rests upon representational metaphor. And it is possible that Gabriel, peace be upon him, brought three forms, in place of each of the qualities a form suited to it; for each of the accidents and the intelligibles has a form suited to it among bodies and sensibles, and by these they are represented in dreams, and indeed in the hereafter. And God knows.

(٤)المراد بالعقل هنا لطيفة ربانية يدرك بها الإنسان حقيقة الأشياء و يميز بها بين الخير و الشر، و الحق و الباطل، و بها يعرف ما يتعلق بالمبدإ و المعاد. و له مراتب بحسب الشدة و الضعف. و الحياء: غريزة مانعة من ارتكاب القبائح و من التقصير في حقوق الحق و الخلق. و الدين: ما به صلاح الناس و رقيهم في المعاش و المعاد من غرائز خلقية و قوانين وضعية.What is meant by al-'aql here is a divine subtlety by which man perceives the reality of things and distinguishes between good and evil, truth and falsehood, and by which he knows what pertains to the origin and the return. It has degrees according to strength and weakness. And al-haya' is an innate disposition preventing the commission of base acts and shortcoming in the rights of the Truth and of creation. And al-din is that by which people's welfare and advancement in this life and the next are secured, whether by natural dispositions or by positive laws.
(٥)لعل المراد بالامر هو التكويني، دون التشريعي. و هو استلزام العقل للحياء و الدين، و تبعيتها له.Perhaps what is meant by the command is the creative, not the legislative — that is, the intellect's entailing modesty and religion, and their following upon it.
