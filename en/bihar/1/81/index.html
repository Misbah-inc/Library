Allama Muhammad Baqir al-Majlisi 1

The Book of Intellect, Knowledge and Ignorance

Chapters on Intellect and Ignorance

Chapter 1: The merit of the intellect and the censure of ignorance

The verses:

al-Baqara: "signs for a people who use their intellect"; and He, exalted, said: "thus does God make clear to you His signs, that perhaps you may understand"; and He, exalted, said: "yet none takes heed save those possessed of understanding."

Al 'Imran: "yet none takes heed save those possessed of understanding"; and He, exalted, said: "We have made clear to you the signs, if you would use your intellect"; and He said: "truly in the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the alternation of night and day, there are signs for those possessed of understanding."

al-Ma'ida: "that is because they are a people who do not use their intellect"; and He, exalted, said: "so fear God, O you possessed of understanding"; and He said: "and most of them do not use their intellect."

al-An'am: "but most of them are ignorant"; and He said: "and surely the abode of the hereafter is better for those who are godfearing — will you not then use your intellect?"

al-Anfal: "truly the worst of creatures in God's sight are the deaf and dumb who do not use their intellect."

Yunus: "can you then make the deaf hear, though they do not use their intellect?"; and He, exalted, said: "and He lays defilement upon those who do not use their intellect."

Hud: "but I see you are a people who are ignorant."

Yusuf: "truly We have sent it down as an Arabic Qur'an, that perhaps you may understand."

al-Ra'd: "only those possessed of understanding take heed."

Ibrahim: "and that those possessed of understanding may take heed."

Ta Ha: "truly in that are signs for those possessed of reason."

al-Nur: "thus does God make clear to you the signs, that perhaps you may understand."

al-Zumar: "truly in that is a reminder for those possessed of understanding."
