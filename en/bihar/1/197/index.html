بيان: يحتمل أن يكون المراد بالملوك ملوك الدين من الأئمة و ولاتهم و يحتمل الأعم فإن طاعة ولاة الجور أيضا تقية من طاعة اللّٰه.

Elucidation: it is possible that what is meant by the kings are the kings of religion, of the Imams and their governors; and it is possible that the more general is meant, for obedience to the governors of tyranny too, by way of dissimulation, is of the obedience of God.

قوله عليه السلام لالتماس الهيئة أي لأن يلاقوهم بهيئة حسنة و يعاشروهم بالمروءة أو لأن يكون لهم عند الناس بسبب معاشرة هؤلاء الأشراف هيئة و مروءة قال الجزري فيه أقيلوا ذوي الهيئات عثراتهم هم الذين لا يعرفون بالشر فيزل أحدهم الزلة و الهيئة صورة الشي ء و شكله و حالته و يريد به ذوي الهيئات الحسنة الذين يلزمون هيئة واحدة و سمتا واحدا و لا تختلف حالاتهم بالتنقل من هيئة إلي هيئة و الأهبة بالضم العدة و الغوائل الشرور و الدواهي و يقال غشي فلانا أي أتاه.

His words, peace be upon him, "seeking comely bearing" — that is, that they may meet them with a fair bearing and associate with them with manliness; or that they may have, in the sight of people, by reason of associating with these nobles, a bearing and a manliness. Al-Jazari said: in it, "overlook the slips of those of comely bearing" — they are those who are not known for evil, and one of them slips a slip. And al-hay'a is the form of a thing and its shape and its state; and he means by it those of fair bearing who keep to one bearing and one manner, and whose states do not vary by shifting from one bearing to another. And al-uhba, with damma, is preparation. And al-ghawa'il are evils and calamities. And one says ghashiya fulanan — that is, he came to him.

«3»- sh, the Sahifa of al-Rida, peace be upon him, from al-Rida, from his forefathers, peace be upon them, who said: The Messenger of God — may God bless him and his family — said: Knowledge (2) is treasuries, and its key (3) is questioning. So ask — God have mercy upon you — for indeed four are rewarded in it: the questioner, and the teacher, and the listener, and the one who loves them (4).
