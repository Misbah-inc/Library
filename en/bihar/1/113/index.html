And awe is fear of God and of His punishment, or of creation, or of the soul and Satan; and the better course is to generalize it, so as to include fear of everything that harms religion or this world. And al-tu'ada, with damma on the ta' and fatha on the hamza or its quiescence, is gravity and deliberateness — that is, not rushing to matters without reflection, for that brings about falling into places of destruction. And in al-Qamus: hadhara his speech, on the pattern of fariha — it was much in error and falsehood; and al-hadhar, with the vowelling, is the much that is bad, or the worthless of speech.

And submission is compliance with God the Exalted in what He commands and forbids; and surrender is compliance with the Imams of truth. And in al-Kafi, opposite surrender is doubt; so what is meant by surrender is acknowledgement of what issues from the prophets and the Imams, peace be upon them, and which minds find hard to accept, as will come in the chapters on knowledge. And what is meant by sufficiency is the sufficiency of the soul and independence from creation — not sufficiency by wealth, for that is mostly with the people of ignorance; and its opposite is poverty towards people and the seeking of recourse to them in affairs. And since inadvertence is an expression of the disappearance of the form from the perceiving faculty and not from the retentive faculty, there was set opposite it recollection, which is the recovering from the retentive faculty. And since forgetfulness is an expression of its disappearance from the retentive faculty also, there was set opposite it retention. And mutual sharing is the making of one's brothers partakers and partners in wealth. And safety is freedom from afflictions, which are faults and blights; and the man of intellect escapes them, since he knows them and knows the way of escaping them, while the ignorant chooses them and falls into them from where he does not know. And al-Shaykh al-Baha'i, may God have mercy on him, said: perhaps what is meant is people's being safe from him,

كما ورد في الحديث المسلم من سلم المسلمون من يده و لسانه.

as has come in the tradition: The Muslim is he from whose hand and tongue the Muslims are safe.

و يراد بالبلاء ابتلاء الناس به و الشهامة ذكاء الفؤاد و توقده.

and what is meant by affliction is people's being afflicted by him. And sagacity is the keenness of the heart and its quickness.
