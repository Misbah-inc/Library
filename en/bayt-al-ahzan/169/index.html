And of her supplication in her illness was: O Living, O Self-Subsisting, by Your mercy I seek help — so help me. O God, remove me far from the Fire and admit me to the Garden and join me to my father Muhammad, may God bless him and his Family. And the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him, would say to her: May God grant you health and keep you. And she would say: O Abu al-Hasan, how swift is the meeting with God! And she charged him to marry Umama bint Abi al-'As, and said: She is my sister's daughter, and she will be tender to my children. [10]
