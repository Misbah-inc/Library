Muhammad the Prophet, may God bless him and his Family, said: He is not of the believers whose neighbour is not safe from his injuries. Whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him not harm his neighbour; and whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him say what is good or keep silent. God Most High loves the good, the forbearing, the self-restraining, and hates the obscene, the foul-mouthed, the importunate beggar. Modesty is of faith, and faith is in the Garden; and obscenity is of foulness, and foulness is in the Fire. [4]

(4)دلائل الإمامة ص 1 .Dala'il al-Imama, p. 1.
